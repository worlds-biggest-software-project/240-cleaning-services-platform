## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **ISSA (International Sanitary Supply Association) CIMS Standard** — Cleaning Industry Management Standard; certification programme for cleaning organisations covering quality systems, customer focus, and environmental stewardship
- **LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) Green Cleaning Requirements** — Specifies cleaning product and process standards for LEED-certified buildings; software must support product-compliance tracking
- **OSHA Hazard Communication Standard (29 CFR 1910.1200)** — Requires Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for cleaning chemicals; software must manage SDS documentation and chemical inventories
- **EPA Safer Choice Programme** — Voluntary certification for cleaning products meeting environmental and safety criteria; increasingly required in institutional contracts
- **HIPAA (in healthcare settings)** — Cleaning service providers in hospitals must handle patient-area cleaning records with appropriate data privacy
- **Living Wage and Fair Work standards** — Growing requirement in commercial-cleaning contracts specifying minimum wage and scheduling-notice requirements; software must support compliance tracking

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The global commercial cleaning services market exceeds $400 billion in revenue; the US residential cleaning market is estimated at over $10 billion. Over 90% of commercial cleaning businesses worldwide have fewer than 10 employees, creating a highly fragmented market with strong demand for affordable, easy-to-deploy software.

**Funding:** Jobber raised over $100 million; Housecall Pro raised over $50 million; ZenMaid and Swept are smaller, founder-led businesses; GorillaDesk raised modest seed funding. The segment attracts PE-backed horizontal FSM platforms rather than specialised VC.

**Pricing Landscape:** Entry-level tools start at $19–$49/month for small owner-operators; mid-market platforms run $100–$500/month for companies with 5–20 cleaners; enterprise or franchise-level solutions involve custom contracts. Payment-processing fees (2.9% + 30¢) add significant per-job costs that buyers scrutinise.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Residential cleaning business owners, commercial janitorial company operations managers, franchise operators, hospitality housekeeping directors, and facilities managers outsourcing cleaning contracts.

**Notable Trends:** Real-time cleaner tracking and proof-of-service becoming baseline customer expectations; digital quality checklists replacing paper inspection forms; online booking and instant quoting increasingly required; growing commercial-cleaning demand from post-pandemic office-return and healthcare facility growth; sustainability requirements (green cleaning product tracking) entering procurement criteria.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- Intelligent scheduling that accounts for cleaner proximity, job duration variability, client preferences, and traffic to build optimal daily routes with minimal manual dispatch effort
- AI-powered quality inspection using photos submitted by cleaners to detect missed areas and flag issues before the client notices, with automatic remediation job creation
- Dynamic pricing engine that adjusts quotes based on property size, service frequency, market rates, and historical job-duration data to maximise margins
- Automated client communication: AI drafts appointment reminders, post-service summaries, and follow-up review requests personalised to each client's communication preferences
- Cleaner retention analytics identifying patterns correlated with turnover and recommending scheduling or workload adjustments to improve staff satisfaction and reduce rehiring costs
